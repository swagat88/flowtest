# Best of Best India - Marketing Copy

## Tagline
"Vote. Back. Win."

## Hero Section
**Headline:** The Arena Where India Decides What's Best

**Subheadline:** Back your favorites with real money. Watch the leaderboard move in real-time. Win big when your pick takes the crown.

## How It Works

### For Voters
1. **Browse Categories** - Explore active arenas and discover nominees
2. **Place Your Bid** - Back your favorite with ₹50-₹10,000
3. **Watch Live** - See rankings update instantly as others bid
4. **Win Prizes** - Top backers share the prize pool when your pick wins

### For Nominees
1. **Get Nominated** - Community members nominate you for a category
2. **Gain Visibility** - Appear on the leaderboard as backers support you
3. **Build Momentum** - Rally your audience to bid and climb ranks
4. **Claim Victory** - Win the category and take home the crown
