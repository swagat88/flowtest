# Best of Best India - Seed Bidders

## Purpose
Seed bidders create initial momentum and demonstrate platform activity. These are trusted community members who place early bids to bootstrap the leaderboard.

## Seed Bidder Profiles

### 1. Tech Enthusiasts (AI Tools Category)
**Profile:** Active in developer communities, follow AI trends, early adopters
**Bid Range:** ₹500 - ₹2,000
**Strategy:** Back 2-3 promising tools early to create diverse leaderboard

**Example Bidders:**
- @techguru_mumbai - 1,200 followers, AI newsletter author
- @devcommunity_delhi - Developer meetup organizer
- @startupbangalore - Startup ecosystem influencer

### 2. Meme Culture Experts (Best Meme Pages Category)
**Profile:** Active on Instagram/Twitter, understand viral content, high engagement
**Bid Range:** ₹200 - ₹1,000
**Strategy:** Back established meme pages to create early competition

**Example Bidders:**
- @memeking_india - 50K followers, meme aggregator
- @viralviralviral - Content strategist
- @humorhub_delhi - Comedy content creator

### 3. Maharashtra Influencers (Instagram Creator Category)
**Profile:** Maharashtra-based creators, strong local following, visual content focus
**Bid Range:** ₹300 - ₹1,500
**Strategy:** Back diverse creators across niches (travel, food, lifestyle, art)

**Example Bidders:**
- @maharashtracreators - Creator collective account
- @mumbaiphotographer - Photography community leader
- @punefoodie - Food and lifestyle influencer

## Seed Bidding Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Day 1-3)
- 5-7 seed bidders per category
- Each places 2-3 bids across different nominees
- Total seed capital: ₹50,000 per category

### Phase 2: Momentum (Day 4-7)
- Seed bidders increase bids on top performers
- Create visible competition on leaderboard
- Encourage organic bidders to join

### Phase 3: Transition (Day 8+)
- Organic bidders overtake seed bidders
- Seed bidders reduce activity
- Platform sustains itself

## Seed Bidder Guidelines
1. **Diversify** - Don't concentrate all bids on one nominee
2. **Be Authentic** - Back tools/pages you genuinely appreciate
3. **Engage** - Comment on nominees you support
4. **Monitor** - Watch the leaderboard and adjust strategy
5. **Transition** - Gradually reduce activity as organic bids grow

## Compensation
- Seed bidders receive platform credits (1.5x bid amount)
- Early access to new categories
- "Founding Backer" badge on profile
- Invitation to exclusive community events
